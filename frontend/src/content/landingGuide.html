WHAT IS UNO NO MERCY?

UNO No Mercy is Mattel's tougher version of UNO, with 168 cards and a rule that knocks you out at 25. You can play UNO No Mercy online right here, with friends or against the computer. Open Mercy is a free card game you play in your browser, built on the UNO No Mercy rules: bigger draw cards, stacking, and a hard limit on how many cards you can hold. It is independent and not affiliated with Mattel.

Play online with friends

Create a private room and send the link, or read out the 6-character code. A room holds up to 20 players, on phones or computers. Voice chat is built in, and the host picks how draw cards stack: official, house or casual.

Play against the computer

Play vs bot is one on one against a computer opponent. Eight bots stand on a ladder, and beating one unlocks the next. Today's deal gives every player the same shuffle, so you can compare results with friends.

How to play against the computer

The rules in one minute

Match the top card by color, number or symbol. Draw cards stack, and whoever cannot answer the stack takes all of it. A 7 swaps hands and a 0 passes every hand along. Hold 25 cards and you are out. Call MERCY! when you are down to one card, or it can cost you 10 cards.

All 168 cards

The deck has 80 number cards, 64 colored action cards and 24 wilds. The ones that change a game are Draw 10, Skip Everyone, Discard All and Color Roulette.

No download, no sign-up

Press PLAY NOW and you are in the lobby with a guest account. There is no email to give, nothing to download and nothing to pay. A free account is optional and only keeps your stats.
