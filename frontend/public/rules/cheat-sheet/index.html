UNO No Mercy rules cheat sheet

One page, every rule.
Full guide with examples: open-mercy.com/rules

Setup

  • Players: 2 to 20. Best with 3 to 6.
  • Deck: 168 cards. The classic 108 plus 60 No Mercy cards.
  • Deal: 7 cards each. The next card starts the discard pile.
  • Goal: empty your hand first, or be the last player not eliminated.

Your turn

  • Play one card matching the top card by color, number or symbol. Wilds go on anything.
  • Can't play? Draw until you can, then play it. House rule: draw one card and move on.

Draw stacking

Hit by a Draw card? Answer with a Draw card of equal or higher value and the whole total passes to the next player. Can't stack: draw the full total and lose your turn. A +10 with a +6 on top is 16 cards.

OfficialEqual or higher only. A +4 cannot go on a +10.
HouseWild +4s stack on any colored Draw card. Colored draws still need equal or higher.
CasualAny Draw card stacks on any Draw card.

Mercy rule

25 cards in hand at any moment and you are out.

Checked after every draw, including mid-penalty. If everyone else is eliminated, the last player left wins, even with cards in hand.

UNO call

  • Call "UNO!" when you go down to one card, before the next player's turn.
  • Caught not calling: draw 2 (printed rule). In Open Mercy an opponent presses CAUGHT and you draw 10.
  • No call needed when one play (like Discard All) takes you straight to zero.

Scoring (optional)

  • Number cards: face value. Action cards: 20. Wild cards: 50.
  • +250 for each player you eliminated with the mercy rule.
  • Keep a running tally across hands. First to 1000 points wins.

Two players

  • Reverse acts as a Skip. Wild Reverse Draw 4: the printed rule makes you draw 4 unless you stack; Open Mercy sends the 4 to your opponent.
  • 0 Rotate is a hand swap. 7 Swap has one target.

Quick strategy

  • Hold your +6 and +10 to answer a stack rather than to open one.
  • Skip Everyone right before you go out: nobody gets a turn to stop you.
  • Color Roulette is a gamble both ways. A color with few cards left in the deck hurts more.
  • A 7 is a defense: swap out of a 20-card hand before the mercy rule finds you.
