Play UNO No Mercy rules online with friends Free, up to 20 players, no download

By alii13 · Last updated 17 November 2026 · Checked against the live game

Open Mercy is a free browser game that plays by UNO No Mercy rules. To play with friends, one person makes a room and sends a link. Everyone else opens it and is at the table, on any phone or computer, with no app to install and no account to make.

Start a room in three steps

  1. Open open-mercy.com and press PLAY NOW. You get a guest account straight away, with no email needed.
  2. In the lobby, pick a stacking mode and press CREATE GAME. That makes a private room that only people with your link or code can enter.
  3. Press SHARE LINK or COPY LINK and send it to your group. When everyone is in the waiting room, press START GAME. You need at least 2 players.

Everyone is dealt 7 cards and the first card is turned over. From there it plays like the card game: match the top card by color, number or symbol, or tap the deck to draw.

How your friends join

Friends open the invite link and land in your room. If someone only has the 6-character room code, they choose Join with code in their own lobby and type it in.

Nobody needs an account or an app. The link opens in the normal browser on iPhone, Android or a computer, and phones and computers can share one room. A room holds up to 20 players. If it is full, the next person sees "Game is full".

Tip: send the link, not the code. A link cannot be mistyped.

Choose how draw cards stack

The host picks one stacking mode before creating the room. It decides which draw cards can be played on top of each other.

ModeWhat stacks
OFFICIALA draw card can be answered only with a draw card of equal or higher value.
HOUSEWild draw cards stack on any draw card. Colored draw cards still need equal or higher value.
CASUALAny draw card stacks on any other draw card.

The stacking rules page shows each mode with worked examples.

Talk while you play

Online rooms have voice chat, and it is optional. Press JOIN VOICE to talk to the table. Nobody hears you until you do, and your browser asks for microphone permission the first time. The host gets a Mute everyone button for when it gets loud.

Rules your group should know

Open Mercy follows the rules of UNO Show 'Em No Mercy. Four of them differ from the classic game:

  • Draw cards stack. A Draw 2 can grow into a far bigger penalty, and whoever cannot answer it takes the whole pile. See stacking.
  • 7 swaps, 0 rotates. Play a 7 to trade hands with any player. Play a 0 and every hand moves one seat. See the 7 and 0 rule.
  • 25 cards and you are out. A hand that reaches 25 cards is eliminated. See the mercy rule.
  • Call MERCY! when you are down to one card. Getting caught without the call costs 10 cards.

The card guide covers all 168 cards, including Draw 10, Skip Everyone, Discard All and Color Roulette.

When a game ends

Each game is one round. It ends when a player plays their last card, or when the 25-card rule has knocked out everyone else. The host then sees REMATCH, which deals a new game in the same room, so stay put if you want another.

A player who is knocked out early can stay and watch. If someone's connection drops, the game keeps their seat and reconnects them on its own.

Nobody around right now?

Quick match seats you at a public table with strangers. Nobody has to host: the cards are dealt shortly after the first person sits down, and up to seven can sit at a table.

Or play alone. Play against the computer on a ladder of eight bots, which is also a good way to learn the cards before your group shows up.

Common questions

Can I play UNO No Mercy online with friends for free?

Yes. Open Mercy plays by UNO No Mercy rules and costs nothing. One person creates a room and shares the link, and up to 20 people play in their browsers. It is an independent game, not the official UNO app.

Do my friends need an account or an app?

No. The invite link opens in a normal browser and gives each player a guest account. There is an iPhone app for anyone who prefers one, but nobody needs it.

How many people can play in one room?

A room holds 2 to 20 players.

Can we play from different devices?

Yes. Phones, tablets and computers can share one room. Everyone opens the same link.

Can we use our own house rules?

The host picks one of three stacking modes: official, house or casual. The rest of the ruleset is the same in every room.

Is this the official UNO game?

No. Open Mercy is an independent game. UNO and UNO Show 'Em No Mercy are trademarks of Mattel, and this project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Mattel.
