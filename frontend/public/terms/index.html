The short version

Play fair, do not wreck the game for other people, and do not pretend the game is yours. The game is provided as is and free to play. If paid content is added, you are buying a licence to use it inside the game, not a thing you own, and it comes with a refund window.

Who can play

Anyone can play the free game. To buy anything, you must be old enough to enter a contract where you live, which in most places means 18. If you are younger, you may buy only with a parent or guardian handling the payment.

The game is not designed for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. See the privacy policy for what is stored.

Your account

Guest play

Pressing play gives you an anonymous account tied to your browser. Clear your browser data and it is gone, along with any stats on it. Convert it to a real account if you want to keep your record.

A real account

You are responsible for keeping access to your email and password. Do not share your account, and do not use someone else's. One person, one account.

Fair play

Automated play is allowed

Open Mercy deliberately exposes the game to software agents, and playing a seat with one is a supported feature rather than cheating. The rules below apply to agents exactly as they apply to people.

What is not allowed

Reading other players' hidden cards, tampering with game state, faking results, flooding the servers, opening rooms or accounts in bulk, attacking the service, or otherwise breaking the game for other people. Doing any of it can cost you your account without notice.

Names and chat

Display names and messages are visible to other players. Keep them free of harassment, slurs, threats, sexual content involving minors, and anything illegal. We can change a name or remove content that breaks this, and repeat offences cost the account.
