Refund policy

Last updated 21 August 2026

The game itself is free, so there is usually nothing to refund. If you buy digital content inside it, you have 14 days to change your mind. Ask and you get your money back.

The short version

Within 14 days of buying, ask for a refund and you get one. No form to fill, no reason required. After 14 days we look at it case by case and still lean towards yes when something went wrong.

What you bought

Purchases in Open Mercy are digital items used inside the game, such as cosmetic card backs and supporter badges. They are delivered to your account instantly. Nothing is downloaded and nothing is shipped, so there is nothing to return: a refund simply reverses the payment and removes the item from your account.

Payments are processed by Dodo Payments as merchant of record. Refunds go back through them to the original payment method. We cannot send a refund anywhere else.

The 14-day window

Inside 14 days

Ask and it is approved. You do not need to explain yourself, and having used the item does not disqualify you.

After 14 days

Judged case by case. If the item never worked, was charged twice, or was bought by someone without permission on your account, you get a refund regardless of how long it has been.

How to ask

  1. Open the feedback button inside the game, or open an issue on GitHub.
  2. Say that you want a refund, and include the email you paid with.
  3. Add the approximate date and amount if you have it. It speeds things up but is not required.

You do not need an order number. We can find the payment from your account email.

How long it takes

Requests are answered within 3 business days. Once approved, the refund is issued immediately on our side, and your bank or card issuer usually takes 5 to 10 business days to show it. The item is removed from your account when the refund is issued.

Cancelling a subscription

If you hold a recurring subscription, you can cancel it at any time and it stops future charges. You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. Cancelling is not the same as a refund: if you also want the last charge back, ask, and the 14-day rule above applies to it.

The exceptions

Where a refund can be refused

Repeated buy-and-refund cycles on the same account, purchases made with a stolen payment method, and accounts closed for cheating or abuse under the terms of service. Nothing else.

Before you file a chargeback

Ask us first. A chargeback takes weeks, freezes the amount while the bank decides, and usually gets the account locked while it is open. A direct request is faster and we have no reason to refuse one inside the window.

Contact

Use the feedback button inside the game, or open an issue on GitHub. Billing questions and refund requests are handled through the same channels.

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. A purchase is always covered by the policy that was published when you bought.
